DeepSeek Harness is growing fast — and so is the ecosystem around it. Today we're launching **oh! dsh**, the community home for everything DeepSeek Harness (DSH).

## What is DeepSeek Harness?

DeepSeek Harness is a Cordis-based agent runtime that composes every capability as a plugin. Whether you're building agents, automations, or content pipelines, DSH turns them into modular, reusable pieces.

## What is oh! dsh?

oh! dsh is a third-party ecosystem site built by the community, for the community. We exist to answer three questions:

1. **Which plugins should I use?** — a curated catalog of the best DSH plugins, organized by what they do
2. **How should I build?** — battle-tested best practices distilled from real-world DSH projects
3. **Where do I start?** — bilingual documentation that gets you productive in minutes

## What you'll find here

- **Curated plugin catalog** — hand-picked DSH plugins with clear descriptions, so you stop guessing and start building
- **Best practices** — composition patterns, security hygiene, and publishing workflows that scale
- **Bilingual docs** — full documentation in English and Chinese
- **Changelog** — every update to the site and the catalog, in one place
