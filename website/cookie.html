1. What cookies are

A cookie is a small text file a website stores on your device, usually containing an identifier and settings. Cookies can be "first party" (set by the site you are visiting) or "third party" (set by another domain). Similar technologies — local storage, pixels and scripts — can serve comparable purposes. This policy covers all of them.

3. Advertising and analytics

At present the Site carries no advertising and no third-party tracking. Should we introduce advertising (for example via Google AdSense), third parties may set cookies to measure ad performance and, with your consent where required, personalise ads. In that case we will update this policy, add a consent mechanism, and you will be able to review or withdraw advertising personalisation through:

  • Google's Ads Settings: adssettings.google.com
  • Network Advertising Initiative: optout.networkadvertising.org
  • European Interactive Digital Advertising Alliance: youronlinechoices.eu

4. How to control cookies

You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings at any time, and most browsers let you clear cookies for a specific site. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may affect Site functionality (for example, forms). Your browser may also send a Do Not Track signal; our analytics respect it. Because the Site currently sets no non-essential cookies, no consent banner is needed today — this page exists to document the Site's actual state and our commitments for the future.

5. Changes and contact

We will update this policy whenever the Site's use of cookies changes, with the revision date at the top of this page. Questions: support@ohdsh.com. Our handling of personal data more broadly is described in the Privacy Policy.
